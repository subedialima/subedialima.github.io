Current research

Spatially filtered approximate nearest neighbor search

Investigating how geographic constraints can be integrated into graph-based semantic retrieval while maintaining high recall and low latency. This work examines spatially informed graph navigation, semantic–geographic relationships, and the trade-offs among recall, throughput and search cost.

Geometric MinHash and polygon similarity

Developing area-based MinHash signatures whose collision probabilities preserve polygon intersection-over-union, with sparsity-aware exponential hashing, compact filter-and-refine methods and bounded rejection sampling to reduce computation.

Parallel and distributed similarity search

Investigating weighted Jaccard candidate pruning, LSH-based partitioning and scalable ANN graph construction. Implementations include C++, MPI and GEOS, with experiments on the Aurora exascale system.

Future research directions

General filter-aware ANN

Adaptive graph navigation and query execution for spatial, temporal, numeric and categorical predicates.

Polygon similarity joins

Sketch-based candidate generation, geometric filtering and parallel verification for GIS data integration.

Compact vector indexing

MinHash-inspired representations for suitable vector domains and hybrid approaches combining sketches, PQ and graph indexes.
