Computer Science · Research

Alima Subedi

Ph.D. researcher at Missouri University of Science and Technology

I study efficient algorithms and systems for similarity search, with a focus on randomized hashing, approximate nearest neighbor search, and geospatial data. My research bridges algorithm design and practical indexing systems for large-scale retrieval.

Selected publicationsGitHub

Research interests

Similarity search & hashing

Randomized sampling, MinHash, Jaccard similarity and compact representations for efficient retrieval.

Spatial & geometric data

Shape similarity, polygon indexing and geometry-aware approximate search.

Vector search systems

Graph-based approximate nearest neighbor search and spatially constrained vector retrieval.
