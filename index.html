About me

Welcome to my homepage!

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at Missouri S&T, advised by Dr. Satish Puri and co-advised by Dr. Md Arifuzzaman. I expect to complete my Ph.D. in May 2027.

My research develops efficient similarity-preserving algorithms and scalable retrieval systems at the intersection of artificial intelligence, data management and geospatial computing.

Research interests

Vector Databases & AI Retrieval

Approximate nearest neighbor search, DiskANN, HNSW, product quantization, filtered vector retrieval and RAG.

Similarity-Preserving Algorithms

MinHash, weighted Jaccard similarity, locality-sensitive hashing, geometric similarity search and polygon joins.

Scalable & Parallel Systems

Distributed ANN, graph construction, parallel algorithms and high-performance computing.

Research collaborations with Microsoft Research and Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory.
